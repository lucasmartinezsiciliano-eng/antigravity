# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Men aged 18-40 who want a precise haircut recommendation without guesswork. They pull out their phone at or before the barbershop. Goal: arrive at the barber knowing exactly what to ask for. Secondary users: barber partners who use the platform to recommend the service to their clients.

## Product Purpose

VISAI analyzes cranial morphology using 468 facial landmarks and AI to generate 3 exact haircut recommendations personalized to the user's face shape. It replaces an expensive professional visagist consultation (50-80€) with a 2-minute mobile analysis for 16.99€. No account required. Photo deleted immediately after analysis.

## Brand Personality

Precise, dark, premium. The tone is confident without being loud. Expert without being cold. The brand speaks like a master barber who never needs to explain himself twice.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dark mode (Linear, Vercel, Raycast aesthetic — too developer-tool)
- AI hype landing pages with gradient text, glowing orbs, hero metrics
- Friendly-casual barbershop branding (warm colors, hand-lettered logos, craft-beer aesthetics)
- "App of everything" cluttered UI
- Any warm beige / cream / sand backgrounds

## Design Principles

1. **Silence earns trust.** Negative space is the primary design material. When in doubt, remove.
2. **Typography carries the weight.** One strong typeface family. Size and weight contrast do all the work that color usually tries to do.
3. **Show the output, not the promise.** Lead with what the analysis actually produces, not adjectives about it.
4. **Precision in every detail.** 468 landmarks. 3 cortes. 2 minutos. Numbers are the voice.
5. **Mobile is the product.** Every layout decision starts at 375px. Desktop is a secondary consideration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. All text ≥ 4.5:1 contrast. Reduced-motion respected for all transitions. Tap targets ≥ 44px.
